# Game Day Prep — Beta 0.4
A separate non-game mode, preserving Crate Crash unchanged apart from a navigation link. Uses a 60-minute clock-relative schedule and manual throw counts. Default high school bullpen cap 20; youth 12, junior 16, adult 20; adjustable downward or within profile maxima. These are coaching examples, not universal safe volumes. Pitch Smart guidance: https://www.mlb.com/pitch-smart/pitching-guidelines — age-specific eligibility, fatigue and rest limits; youth fastballs/changeups only. Coach must confirm familiar pitches and readiness. No required long-toss distance or throwing volume. No background service worker, device permissions, network data, or persistent storage.

## J-Bands and PlyoCare revision
60-minute schedule: readiness 3, dynamic 7, bands 8, optional PlyoCare 9, catch 5, extension 5, hydration 3, mound 14, transition 6. Reduced 16 or full 32 weighted throws, default none; available only for already-adapted high school (15+) and adult profiles by coach approval. Reverse 1500g/1000g (legacy 2000g optional); pivot 1000g/450g; step backs/drop steps/walking windups 450/225/150/100g. Full uses 5 per heavy ball and 1 per lighter ball per drill; reduced uses 3 per heavy and walking windups only (1 each). These are Smart Bullpen adaptations, not a verified optimal Driveline pregame prescription. No high intent. All actual plyo throws recorded separately. Jaeger 11 movements with reduced pregame volume; official conditioning sheet generally specifies 25 reps and timed stretches, while Driveline programs use 10 reps per band movement. ## Exercise video guide — beta 0.5
Click-to-load public video embeds; no autoplay and no video provider requests until the athlete/coach chooses to load a video. Only Game Day Prep permits frames from youtube-nocookie.com and player.vimeo.com; arcade CSP unchanged. Videos are unloaded when the phase changes, the routine ends or another video is opened. The clock continues from the actual game time. Original links are always available in a new tab. The video library is filtered by selected PlyoCare plan and age profile. Technique references are not identical to the programmed dose; some providers demonstrate high-intent variants that should not be performed pregame. No files rehosted. Video providers can collect data; privacy-enhanced embeds do not eliminate tracking. Videos are third-party content and may be unavailable or show ads/recommendations.
