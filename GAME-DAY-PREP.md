# Game Day Prep — Beta 0.4
A separate non-game mode, preserving Crate Crash unchanged apart from a navigation link. Uses a 60-minute clock-relative schedule and manual throw counts. Default high school bullpen cap 20; youth 12, junior 16, adult 20; adjustable downward or within profile maxima. These are coaching examples, not universal safe volumes. Pitch Smart guidance: https://www.mlb.com/pitch-smart/pitching-guidelines — age-specific eligibility, fatigue and rest limits; youth fastballs/changeups only. Coach must confirm familiar pitches and readiness. No required long-toss distance or throwing volume. No background service worker, device permissions, network data, or persistent storage.

## J-Bands and PlyoCare revision
60-minute schedule: readiness 3, dynamic 7, bands 8, optional PlyoCare 9, catch 5, extension 5, hydration 3, mound 14, transition 6. Reduced 16 or full 32 weighted throws, default none; available only for already-adapted high school (15+) and adult profiles by coach approval. Reverse 1500g/1000g (legacy 2000g optional); pivot 1000g/450g; step backs/drop steps/walking windups 450/225/150/100g. Full uses 5 per heavy ball and 1 per lighter ball per drill; reduced uses 3 per heavy and walking windups only (1 each). These are Smart Bullpen adaptations, not a verified optimal Driveline pregame prescription. No high intent. All actual plyo throws recorded separately. Jaeger 11 movements with reduced pregame volume; official conditioning sheet generally specifies 25 reps and timed stretches, while Driveline programs use 10 reps per band movement.
