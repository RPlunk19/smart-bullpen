# Beta 0.6 — Crate Crash Adventure
Home mode cards, high-contrast Game Day Prep link, five original vector world backgrounds and matching crates, 20 campaign levels, anonymous device-local progression. The four levels per world must be cleared sequentially. Goal reached = level ends immediately; below goal at cap or coach stop = no new unlock. Goal thresholds range from 200 to 1200. Ten pitches is the maximum per campaign mission, coach can lower it; an impossible point goal is flagged. Free play remains 1–30 pitches and retains original behavior. Accept the result to save; undo last pitch before acceptance. One shared campaign per browser, no player identity or pitch logs saved, no cross-device sync. Stars: one for clear, two at >=50% exact command, three at >=70%; no stars for failure. No automatic next level or replay, no extra pitches. Keep throwing within coach plan across all missions. Replay only in a planned throwing session. Original SVG art (not finished commercial 3D assets). Existing Game Day Prep page untouched. Core campaign assertions and JS parse passed; browser testing and independent security review outstanding. Test opening each mode, map lock, goal early stop, miss cap, undo winning pitch, accept/unlock, refresh persistence, mobile orientation and reduced motion. Run node tests/campaign.test.cjs and node tests/engine.test.cjs.

Five themed 3D environments (palms and platforms, crystal formations, ruins, volcanic rock and embers, sky structures), rotating raised-plank crates and gem/fragment hit animation. SVG scene backdrop and prize art retained. No external graphics engine, textures, models, network calls or new storage. Classic graphics toggle and reduced-motion support; capped device pixel ratio and approximately 30 fps; animation suspended outside play or when tab hidden. World-specific synthesized hit notes and world/championship celebration. Existing 20 level goals, early completion, scoring, coach reports, pitch caps and device-local progression unchanged. World trophy awarded only after final level result is accepted. Geometry unit assertions and JavaScript parsing performed; physical rendering/performance, browser fallback, sound, reduced motion and independent security review outstanding. Test all five worlds, hit/miss/undo, classic toggle, screen orientation, low-power phone, tab visibility, reduced motion and level/world acceptance. Artwork is procedural low-poly 3D, not commercial-quality textured 3D.
## Beta 0.8 — Player Clubhouse
32 local nickname-only profiles, individual campaign maps, original layered vector baseball avatar, seven cosmetic categories, 30 items (including starter items), six skin-tone options, jersey numbers, trophy shelf. Gear unlocks through accepted level clears, a five-hit combo, a successful pitch after a miss, or five three-star levels. No points currency, purchases, randomized paid rewards, extra pitches, or gameplay advantage. Rewards previewed on result and committed only on acceptance, so undo cannot save an unearned result. Existing anonymous progress migrates to Legacy Player when first initialized; old local key retained as backup, not overwritten. All profiles visible to anyone using browser, no authentication, server sync, names beyond fictional nicknames, or raw pitch logs saved. Delete requires confirmation; final profile cannot be deleted. Storage failure falls back to session memory. Avatar art is vector, not a 3D player model. Gameplay 3D renderer and Game Day Prep untouched. Test profile isolation, migration, locked gear, equip, persistence, delete confirmation, free-play achievement, result correction, replay, mobile display and privacy/security before athlete use.
