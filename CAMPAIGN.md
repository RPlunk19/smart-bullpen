## Beta 0.14 — Accuracy power-ups
Human fighters only: third consecutive exact hit grants a two-incoming-turn shield (including the immediate rebuttal; a miss consumes a turn). Halves calculated incoming damage, rounded up, after existing wear/weak/guard/stagger calculations. Fifth consecutive exact hit restores up to 15 HP before the rebuttal, never above max HP or from zero HP. Rewards trigger once per milestone per streak; a non-exact pitch resets streak but does not cancel remaining shield. New shield refreshes to two, not stacked. Both local human duel players follow same rules, robot never earns combo rewards. Existing knockout, cap, specialist, wardrobe and persistence rules retained; no extra pitches. HUD shows streak, shield and reward notifications; results include power-up, healing and prevented HP totals. Undo restores reward and random state. Tested targeted shield, expiration, healing, undo, duel, and 3750 seeded simulated solo matches (19709 turns); syntax and literal DOM IDs checked. Real-device rendering and athlete balance testing outstanding.
