# Beta 0.26.2
Coach-only weekly calendar linked from Today and Assignments, player/team schedule entries for game/practice/lesson, reusable seven-day templates up to 12 weeks, preview and explicit approval, no overwriting existing assignments. Future training flags based on logged official pitches, existing nonthrowing plans, scheduled events/game tomorrow, current workload reference and coach ceiling. Future games are uncertain; no assumed pitch counts, no future ACWR projection or automatic clearance. Player read-only seven-day view and home event hint. Existing coach data is preserved by shared sanitizer. No automatic changes to approved plans: conflicts are flagged live. Local storage and navigation-only role separation remain.
Competition remains separate from an assignment. A scheduled game during known pitching rest flags pitching eligibility review, not a prohibition on attending. A template training day on an outside event becomes a no-additional-app-pitching rest assignment (coach can deselect); no automatic competition participation assignment.
