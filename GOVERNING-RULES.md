# Beta 0.26.2b.5
Selectable source-linked rule references; Pitch Smart default. IHSA published reference requires current-season confirmation, IESA Jan 2026, Little League league age, UIL single-day bands, American Legion 2026 bands. USSSA, PONY, Babe Ruth/Cal Ripken and other are manual review. Not a complete eligibility engine; appearance caps, finish-batter exceptions, series totals, catcher rules and multiple affiliations require official verification. Rest day computed from logged official pitches, not bullpen throws. Existing safety restrictions remain independent. Unknown rules use conservative four-day app hold, not official rest. Browser testing pending.
