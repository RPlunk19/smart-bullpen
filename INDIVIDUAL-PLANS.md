# Beta 0.26.2a
Individual coaching focus based on up to ten recent detailed sessions, familiar arsenal, development preference, and minimum ten observed attempts per target/pitch. Goals select appropriate games; no additional pitch volume. Coach acknowledgment is specific to current plan, workload and schedule snapshot; any changed daily history or plan invalidates review. Legacy/manual training assignments require explicit calendar review. Player warning and start gate across all four games, including Sniper tournament launch/resume. Hard restrictions cannot be acknowledged away: logged pitching rest, exceeded official limit, same-day official outing, assigned nonthrowing day or reached coach ceiling. Recovery/rest remain accessible. Advisory flags may be reviewed and retained, subject to ordinary coach approval. Local beta only; no remote notification, true role authentication, enforcement of actual physical activity, or continuous mid-session checks. Future approvals expire when daily history shifts. No medical clearance.
Coach Today dashboard links directly to pending player assignment review. Guided bullpen warm-up also checks launch eligibility because it includes throwing. Calendar approvals are local; rolling daily snapshot changes require a fresh coach review, even when the resulting recommendation is unchanged.
