# Beta 0.24 — Simulated At-Bats
Solo or local head-to-head, one to three innings, shared fictional nine-hitter lineup, clean inning / inherited runners / bases-loaded scenario, three difficulty levels, coach-approved arsenal, 10/15/20/30/50 pitch caps per pitcher. Call pitch type and intended location before recording actual location (nine strike-zone and four chase zones). Outcomes depend on fictional batter preferences, actual location, count and repetition; illustrative probabilities, not empirically calibrated. Simplified base advancement, walks, fouls, strikeouts, hits and outs. Head-to-head compares runs allowed after both finish their assigned innings; ties are draws, incomplete games have no winner. Undo preserves random draws for correction. Career totals and shared profile victory animation; no new cosmetics in this release. No live pitch tracking or third-party data. Browser and athlete testing required.
Pitch calls require explicit confirmation before actual-location scoring becomes active; a call can be changed before throwing. Decision feedback distinguishes a risky intended spot from an execution miss. Two-player incomplete innings do not produce a competitive winner.
## Beta 0.24.1
Original procedural Canvas2D broadcast animation: pitcher delivery, ball flight to actual recorded location, batter reaction, wide field view and outcome-driven base paths. Cinematic/fast/off and skip; reduced motion bypasses animation. No external video or assets. The engine result is recorded once before the replay; score entry locked until completion. Art is stylized, not motion captured. Browser rendering remains unverified.
## Beta 0.25
Lazy local WebGL stadium, existing skinned Clubhouse pitcher and fictional batter, procedural baseball poses, low-poly fielders/runners, two broadcast cameras, inherited runners at idle, classic fallback and graphics-off controls. This is stylized original motion, not motion capture or validated biomechanics. Shared release branding and navigation polish. Existing game engines unchanged.
## Beta 0.25.1
Eight fictional teams, 72 named batters with twelve tendency archetypes, distinct team colors, varied attributes, contextual scouting, selectable solo opponent, opposing teams or common lineup in head-to-head, and optional shuffled order (shared opponent uses same order). Original illustrative tendencies, not actual MLB data. Legacy engine calls without teams retain original lineup. Selected team uniforms override pitcher jersey/hat color during this mode only; no profile equipment is changed.
## Beta 0.25.2
Five per-player development levels independent of opponent difficulty, approved pitch repertoire, and workload cap. Foundations disables pitch-type preferences/repetition, and every single-pitch arsenal disables them at all levels. Repeated actual locations can make a repeated intended spot predictable; location and count still matter. Local profile remembers development/arsenal when opted in, but requires fresh approval. Contextual scouting and report depth vary by development. New career aggregates separate level/difficulty; prior totals retained, no retroactive categories or ranked leaderboard. Head-to-head mixed development settings are not directly comparable.
