# Beta 0.24 — Simulated At-Bats
Solo or local head-to-head, one to three innings, shared fictional nine-hitter lineup, clean inning / inherited runners / bases-loaded scenario, three difficulty levels, coach-approved arsenal, 10/15/20/30/50 pitch caps per pitcher. Call pitch type and intended location before recording actual location (nine strike-zone and four chase zones). Outcomes depend on fictional batter preferences, actual location, count and repetition; illustrative probabilities, not empirically calibrated. Simplified base advancement, walks, fouls, strikeouts, hits and outs. Head-to-head compares runs allowed after both finish their assigned innings; ties are draws, incomplete games have no winner. Undo preserves random draws for correction. Career totals and shared profile victory animation; no new cosmetics in this release. No live pitch tracking or third-party data. Browser and athlete testing required.
